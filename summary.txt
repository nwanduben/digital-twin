MOVING INTO AI
In November 2025, a friend introduced me to automation, and that led me into conversational AI. From March to August 2026, I worked remotely as a QA Analyst for Conversational AI at VoiceROI. I reviewed calls handled by AI voice agents for six clients across dental and health, finance, wellness and entertainment, spotted failures, and fed clear issues back to the prompt team so the agents could be improved. I also did QA through the clients' migration of their agents from Vapi to Telnyx.

MY PROUDEST MOMENT
At VoiceROI, I built a call-bucketing tool. Each client could have several AI agents, and on a busy day a single client's agents might handle around 1,500 calls combined (one agent alone could take about 900), with most of them voicemails. Reviewing meant listening to everything, one by one. I built a Python tool with Claude Code that automatically sorts calls into categories using the clients' GoHighLevel reporting data, and flags uncertain calls for manual review. That let me skip the noise and focus on the conversations that actually needed QA.
